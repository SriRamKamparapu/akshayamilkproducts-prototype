Our Journey • Tradition & Purity

From Godavari's Lush Pastures Directly to Your Table

Rooted in Samalkot, amidst the fertile delta plains of East Godavari, Andhra Pradesh, Akshaya Milk Products was founded on a singular commitment: unadulterated dairy goodness. We partner directly with generational dairy farmers whose cows and buffaloes graze on natural green pastures. Our traditional curd setting in earthenware, hand-churned white butter (venna), and brass-cauldron simmered golden ghee (neyyi) preserve the ancient dairy heritage of Andhra Pradesh.

Fresh Milk Sourcing
Morning milk collected at dawn from Samalkot farmer collectives with zero chilling delays.
Hand-Churned Butter
Cultured whole curd hand-churned using wooden bilona into velvety white butter (Venna).
Golden Desi Ghee
Slow-simmered on gentle fire in open brass cauldrons for granular texture and golden aroma.
Artisanal Dairy Sweets
Master halwais craft fresh Palakova, Kova Rolls, and pedas using 100% pure condensed milk.
🥛
100% Pure & Hygienic
Zero synthetic milk, starch, or preservatives. Daily quality testing.
❄️
Insulated Cold Chain
Delivered below 4°C in thermal insulated crates for live probiotics.
🧈
Ancestral Bilona Method
Curd-churned butter simmered traditionally without chemical industrial separators.
📍
Local Samalkot Pride
